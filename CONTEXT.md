# Practice room

Lomme supplies a small synthesized band for repeating and exploring musical
changes while a person practices.

## Language

**Arrangement**:
A complete practice setup: its chart, home key, tempo, accompaniment, mix,
practice options, and descriptive information. A personal arrangement keeps its
identity when renamed.
_Avoid_: Song (which suggests a recording), name-as-identity

**Preset**:
A built-in arrangement offered as an unchanging starting point for practice.

**Practice session**:
The current working arrangement, including edits that have not been saved to
the personal library.
_Avoid_: Saved arrangement when referring to unsaved working changes

**Chart**:
An ordered sequence of repeating four-beat bars; a paired bar gives each of two
chords two beats.
_Avoid_: Chord list when bar boundaries matter

**Personal library**:
The collection of arrangements a person explicitly saves or imports. Different
arrangements may share the same name.

**Track file**:
A portable arrangement without personal identity or playback position.
_Avoid_: Recording, audio file

**Trade fours**:
A practice mode in which keys alternate four bars on and four bars off, across
chart repeats.
